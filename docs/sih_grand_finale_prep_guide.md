# 🏆 OrbitGuard: Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Grand Finale Preparation Guide

If you get selected for the **SIH Grand Finale (36-Hour Non-Stop Hackathon)**, the evaluation structure changes significantly. During the finals, judges conduct **3 to 4 sequential evaluation rounds (mentoring/judging cycles)**. In each round, they will test your current version and then **give you "surprise requirements" (incremental features)** that you must code and deploy before the next round (usually within 6–8 hours).

For a highly advanced Space Situational Awareness (SSA) system like **OrbitGuard**, here is exactly what you should expect to work on, code, and present during those 36 hours.

---

## 🚨 1. Expected "Surprise Requirements" from SIH Judges

During the evaluations, judges will push you to transition this from a "working prototype" to an "enterprise-ready system." They are highly likely to ask for:

### A. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Multi-Tenancy
* **The Ask:** *"Currently, anyone can view or maneuver any satellite. Implement strict agency separation."*
* **What you will code:** Set up secure logins where:
  - **ISRO operators** can only coordinate maneuvers for Indian satellites (e.g., Cartosat, GSAT).
  - **SpaceX operators** only see Starlink assets.
  - **USSPACECOM/In-Space** has global monitoring and auditing privileges.
* **Why it matters:** Demonstrates security and commercial viability for multi-operator scenarios.

### B. Real Radar Ground Station Mocking
* **The Ask:** *"Show me how the Ground Refinement engine receives actual measurements in real time, not just static inputs."*
* **What you will code:** Build a lightweight mock telemetry generator script that pushes simulated radar ranging measurements over a **TCP socket or WebSocket** into the backend every few seconds, updating the Cesium globe dynamically.
* **Why it matters:** Proves real-world sensor integration capability.

### C. CCSDS Standards Integration
* **The Ask:** *"Your maneuver portal coordinates plans, but how do you transfer files to actual space agencies?"*
* **What you will code:** Add actual parser/export features for standard CCSDS formats:
  - **OEM (Orbit Ephemeris Message)** files.
  - **CDM (Conjunction Data Message)** files instead of generic JSON.
* **Why it matters:** Shows compliance with international space flight protocols.

## ⚡ 2. Technical Scaling Challenges (36-Hour Sprints)

To win the grand prize, you must demonstrate performance scaling and optimization under pressure. Focus your coding sprints on:

### A. Stress Testing & Parallelization (Massive Scale)
* **The Problem:** The prototype runs on ~100 satellites. The actual space catalog has **35,000+ tracked objects**.
* **Your Task:** Optimize the SGP4 propagation loop using Python's `multiprocessing` or `concurrent.futures` to propagate 20,000 satellites in parallel. Show a live benchmark speed comparison on the Diagnostics HUD.
* **Why it matters:** Proves your physics backend won't crash when loaded with real catalog data.

### B. 3D Relative Motion Visualization (RIC/Hill Frame)
* **The Problem:** The B-Plane and maneuver planners are currently shown on flat 2D SVG plots.
* **Your Task:** Use CesiumJS/Resium to draw the **3D relative motion trajectory** (the Clohessy-Wiltshire loop) directly around the primary satellite on the 3D globe. This allows judges to visually watch the satellite execute its avoidance maneuver in 3D space.
* **Why it matters:** Outstanding visual impact that immediately captures judge attention.

### C. Advanced Solar Storm Forecast Model
* **The Problem:** The solar storm warning is based on NOAA SWPC static indexes.
* **Your Task:** Write a simple machine learning model (e.g. an LSTM or simple linear regression in scikit-learn) that runs on the backend, ingests F10.7 history, and *forecasts* geomagnetic storms 3 hours in advance, triggering preemptive warning states.
* **Why it matters:** Adds "AI-powered forecasting" credibility to the platform.

## 🧪 3. Data Accuracy Testing & Optimization Strategy

Data accuracy is the most critical metric for space safety operations. During the finale, you must show a clear verification plan and write optimization layers to prove OrbitGuard operates at enterprise grade:

### A. Ground-Truth Data Verification (How to Test)
* **Precise Ephemeris Comparison:** Compare Cartesian coordinates generated by your SGP4 propagator against high-precision orbital ephemerides (from ILRS laser ranging or GNSS ground-station data). Positional error over 24 hours must be validated and bounded.
* **Analytical Foster vs. Monte Carlo Conjunction Probability:** Verify the analytical Foster 2D Probability of Collision ($P_c$) against a 10,000-run Monte Carlo simulation to prove calculation convergence (error margin $\le 1\%$).
* **NOAA SWPC API Regression Checks:** Write local automated tests verifying NOAA indices caching pipelines to prevent API drift.

### B. High-Fidelity Physics Upgrades (How to Optimize)
* **$J_2$ to $J_4$ Spherical Harmonics:** Model Earth as an oblate spheroid in your RK4 lunar/cislunar propagator. Implementing the equatorial bulge ($J_2$) prevents compounding orbit precession errors.
* **Extended Kalman Filtering (EKF) & Covariance Shrinkage:** Implement an EKF that ingests successive ground sensor observations (range, azimuth, elevation) to dynamically update state vectors and compress the covariance uncertainty ellipse (reducing conjunction false alarms).
* **Dynamic Drag Updating:** Connect live Aditya-L1 solar flux parameters to dynamically update the satellite ballistic coefficient ($B^*$), capturing transient thermospheric expansion drag accurately.

## 🏆 4. Master Presentation & Defense Strategies

Use the built-in presentation aids of OrbitGuard to secure maximum marks:

### 1. Air-Gapped Local Deployment
* **The Strategy:** SIH finale venues often have terrible internet connectivity. Since OrbitGuard features a **local Llama 3.2 model (via Ollama)** and **offline database fallbacks**, you can run the entire system (including LLM generation) completely offline.
* **How to Pitch:** Highlight to the judges that the system is fully secure, containerized, and capable of operating in a classified, completely air-gapped command room.

### 2. Hands-Free Auto-Tour Execution
* **The Strategy:** In the final round, judges evaluate dozens of teams and are mentally exhausted. **Do not explain slides.**
* **How to Pitch:** Sit the judges down, click the **"▶ HACKATHON DEMO TOUR"** button, and let the automated 3D flight and live typing show off the system hands-free. While the tour runs, explain your engineering choices.

### 3. Astro-Physics Defense
* **The Strategy:** If a judge questions the authenticity of your physics (e.g. *"Did you actually calculate this, or is it hardcoded?"*).
* **How to Pitch:** Open the **ℹ Architecture & Q&A drawer** and point directly to the equations. Show them the live-updating SGP4 calculation throughput rates on the **Platform Diagnostics HUD** in real-time.
